Bible widgets for iPhone: how to choose the right one

There are many Bible widget and Christian widget apps for iPhone, and they look alike in the App Store. These seven questions help you find a Bible verse widget you'll actually keep on your phone.

1. Does it work on the Lock Screen, or only the Home Screen?

The Lock Screen is where you look most: every time you check the time or a notification. Some apps only offer Home Screen widgets. Look for both, and for more than one size: a small square and a wide one on the Home Screen, and a spot under the clock on the Lock Screen.

2. How often does the verse change, and can you choose?

A verse that never changes fades into the background. One that changes every few minutes is hard to remember. The best apps let you choose: every hour, a few times a day, once a day, or one verse that stays put while you memorize it.

3. Can you steer which verses you see?

Random verses can land on a list of names. Look for themes, such as hope, peace, anxiety, forgiveness or the words of Jesus, or a way to pick verses yourself.

4. Which Bible translations does it have?

Make sure the translation you read is there. Many apps include the King James Version (KJV). Modern public-domain translations such as the World English Bible (WEB) and the Berean Standard Bible (BSB) are easier to read, and the American Standard Version (ASV) sits in between.

5. Does it work offline?

A widget that needs the internet can go blank on a plane or with a weak signal. Apps that keep the Bible on your phone always have a verse ready.

6. Does it need an account, and what does it collect?

A widget shouldn't need your email. Check the App Privacy section on the app's App Store page: it lists what the app collects and whether it's linked to you.

7. What's free, and what's paid?

Many widget apps are free to download and charge a subscription for some features. That's fair, but it should be clear before you start. Read the App Store page and the in-app price screen, check whether there's a free trial, and know how to cancel (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).

Questions

Are there free Bible widgets for iPhone?

Some apps offer free widgets, and others include them in a subscription. In Narrow Gate, reading the Bible is free and the widgets are part of the subscription. For a completely free option, save a picture of a verse as your wallpaper.

What are Christian widgets for iPhone?

Christian widgets show a Bible verse, a prayer or a reminder on your iPhone's Lock Screen or Home Screen, so you see Scripture every time you pick up your phone.

What is the best Bible verse widget for iPhone?

The best one is the one you'll keep: look for a Lock Screen widget, a choice of how often the verse changes, your translation, offline use, no account, and clear pricing.

Can a Bible widget go on the iPhone Lock Screen?

Yes. Since iOS 16, apps can offer Lock Screen widgets. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, Lock Screen, then Add Widgets.

Do Bible widgets work offline?

Some do. Apps that keep the Bible text on your phone, like Narrow Gate, show a verse without a connection.

Why does my Lock Screen widget look grey or see-through?

iPhone draws every Lock Screen widget in a frosted, see-through style to match your wallpaper. Apps can't change that.
