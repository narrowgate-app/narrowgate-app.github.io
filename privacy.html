Privacy Policy — Narrow Gate

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Short version: Narrow Gate has no accounts, no servers of its own, and no analytics on

what you read or pray. Your choices stay on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your settings — chosen apps, translation, prayer books, focus, church background, rhythm,

reminders, saved prayers, and your streak — are stored on your device only, in the app's

private storage. They are never uploaded.

  • Reminders and the prayer notification are local notifications scheduled on your phone.

Nothing is sent from a server.

  • The apps you choose are handled by Apple's Screen Time framework. Apple never tells

Narrow Gate which apps you picked; the app receives only anonymous tokens it cannot read.

This data cannot leave your device and is never shared, by design of the framework and by

Apple's developer agreement.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm your subscription status.

RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user identifier and your purchase receipt — no name,

email, or usage data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for their handling of that data.

What we do not do

  • No account, email, or sign-in.
  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no data brokers.
  • No collection of which apps you use, how long, or what you read.

Contact

Questions about privacy: narrowgateapp@icloud.com

Changes

We will update this page if the app's data practices change, and note the date above.
