Privacy Policy — Narrow Gate

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Narrow Gate (called Prayer Gate until November 2026) is made by Morning Harbor LLC, a Texas limited liability company ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Short version: Narrow Gate has no accounts and no servers of its own. What you read, pray and save stays on your phone. We count anonymous app events, such as which features get used, to make the app better. They are never linked to you, and never say which verses you read or which apps you lock.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your settings — chosen apps, translation, prayer books, focus, church background, rhythm, reminders, saved prayers, and your streak — are stored on your device only, in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded. (Some choices, like which translation you picked, are also counted anonymously; see below.)
  • Reminders and the prayer notification are local notifications scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent from a server.
  • The apps you choose are handled by Apple's Screen Time framework. Apple never tells Narrow Gate which apps you picked; the app receives only anonymous tokens it cannot read. This data cannot leave your device and is never shared, by design of the framework and by Apple's developer agreement.
  • Your reading record (time spent reading, verses, chapters, prayers per day) is backed up to your own iCloud account through Apple, so it survives a new phone. We cannot see it.

Anonymous app statistics

We use PostHog to count anonymous events so we can see which parts of the app help people and which don't. They are:

  • Setup: which setup screens were shown, the goals picked, and the choices made there (translation, focus topics, lock mode).
  • Features used: which tabs are opened, widget taps (and the widget's size), opening the Bible reader or Scroll, how many verses were swiped in a Scroll visit, that a verse was saved or shared (not which verse), widget and wallpaper settings, translation changes, and setting up the lock (the mode and how many apps were chosen, never which apps).
  • Prayer: that a prayer started or finished, and its length.
  • Subscriptions: whether the paywall or an offer was shown, closed or led to a purchase, and which plan.
  • App opens, installs and updates.

Each event also carries basic phone details: the app version, phone model, iOS version, language and region setting, and time zone. Each install gets a random ID that is not linked to your name, email, device identifiers or advertising ID.

We do not store your internet (IP) address or your location. We do not record your screen, the text of what you read or save, or which apps you lock. (Before November 16, 2026, PostHog also saved the internet address and the approximate city it pointed to; we have turned that off.) PostHog stores this data in the United States. See PostHog's privacy policy for their handling of it.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm your subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user identifier and your purchase receipt — no name, email, or usage data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for their handling of that data.

What we do not do

  • No account, email, or sign-in.
  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no data brokers.
  • No collection of which apps you use or how long, and never which verses you read.
  • No location and no internet (IP) address stored.
  • No session recordings and no advertising identifier.

Children

Narrow Gate is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app collects none from anyone.
