Narrow Gate — Support

How do I get past the block screen?

Tap Pray. A notification from Narrow Gate appears at the top of your screen; tap it and

Narrow Gate opens. Tap Pray now, read until the ring fills, and tap Continue. Your app will

open. If the notification doesn't appear, check that notifications for Narrow Gate are

allowed (iOS Settings → Notifications → Narrow Gate) and that a Focus mode isn't hiding it.

I picked a category and now every app in it has a prayer.

In Apple's picker, the circle next to a category selects the whole group. Open Narrow Gate →

Settings → Apps with a prayer, uncheck the category, then tap the arrow (›) beside it and

choose specific apps.

Screen Time access is off / the app says it needs permission again.

Settings (iOS) → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access → Narrow Gate → on. Or open

Narrow Gate and follow the prompt.

Choosing how often you pray. Narrow Gate → Settings → Select your mode. Light: once in the morning. Steady: three times a day (5 AM, noon, 6 PM). Strong: every hour.

Praying for longer. Narrow Gate → Settings → Rhythm → Prayer length.

Daily reminders. Narrow Gate → Settings → Reminders → Remind me to pray at….

Turning it off. Narrow Gate → Settings → Apps with a prayer → uncheck everything. Or

delete the app; iOS removes the block with it.

Subscription questions. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Restore a purchase in Narrow Gate → Settings → Restore purchases.
