Narrow Gate: Scripture where you already look

A Bible app for iPhone that puts God's Word on your Lock Screen, in front of the apps that take your time, and one verse at a time in your hand.

"Enter by the narrow gate." Matthew 7:13

Bible verse widgets

A verse on your Lock Screen and Home Screen that changes every hour, every day, or stays on the one you love.

A prayer before your apps

Pick the apps that eat your time. Opening one asks for a short prayer first.

The Bible, one verse at a time

Swipe up through the whole Bible like a feed. KJV, WEB, BSB and ASV, offline.

Built for a quieter phone

  • No account and no sign-in. Your reading stays on your iPhone and your own iCloud.
  • Four full Bibles inside the app, so everything works without a connection.
  • Verse wallpapers you can save in seconds.
