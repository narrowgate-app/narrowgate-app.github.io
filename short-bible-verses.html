Short Bible verses (KJV)

A short verse is easy to remember, easy to carry through the day, and short enough to read in a glance at your phone. Here are 40 short Bible verses from the King James Version, grouped by what you need.

Short Bible verses for strength

  • "I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me." Philippians 4:13
  • "God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble." Psalm 46:1
  • "My help cometh from the LORD, which made heaven and earth." Psalm 121:2
  • "The LORD shall fight for you, and ye shall hold your peace." Exodus 14:14
  • "The LORD will give strength unto his people; the LORD will bless his people with peace." Psalm 29:11
  • "What shall we then say to these things? If God be for us, who can be against us?" Romans 8:31

Short Bible verses for peace and comfort

  • "What time I am afraid, I will trust in thee." Psalm 56:3
  • "Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you." 1 Peter 5:7
  • "I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, LORD, only makest me dwell in safety." Psalm 4:8
  • "Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee." Isaiah 26:3
  • "Now the Lord of peace himself give you peace always by all means. The Lord be with you all." 2 Thessalonians 3:16
  • "The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit." Psalm 34:18

Short Bible verses about hope

  • "It is of the LORD’s mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not." Lamentations 3:22
  • "They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness." Lamentations 3:23
  • "I wait for the LORD, my soul doth wait, and in his word do I hope." Psalm 130:5
  • "Be of good courage, and he shall strengthen your heart, all ye that hope in the LORD." Psalm 31:24
  • "Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path." Psalm 119:105

Short Bible verses about faith and trust

  • "The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want." Psalm 23:1
  • "Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding." Proverbs 3:5
  • "Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen." Hebrews 11:1
  • "Commit thy way unto the LORD; trust also in him; and he shall bring it to pass." Psalm 37:5
  • "Commit thy works unto the LORD, and thy thoughts shall be established." Proverbs 16:3
  • "For with God nothing shall be impossible." Luke 1:37

Short Bible verses about love

  • "He that loveth not knoweth not God; for God is love." 1 John 4:8
  • "We love him, because he first loved us." 1 John 4:19
  • "Let all your things be done with charity." 1 Corinthians 16:14
  • "This is my commandment, That ye love one another, as I have loved you." John 15:12

In the King James Version, "charity" means love.

Short Bible verses about joy, praise and thanks

  • "O give thanks unto the LORD; for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever." Psalm 136:1

  • "Pray without ceasing." 1 Thessalonians 5:17
  • "In every thing give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you." 1 Thessalonians 5:18

Short verses in the words of Jesus

  • "Let not your heart be troubled: ye believe in God, believe also in me." John 14:1
  • "I am the good shepherd: the good shepherd giveth his life for the sheep." John 10:11

The shortest verse in the Bible

In the King James Version, the shortest verse is John 11:35: "Jesus wept." Two words, said at the tomb of His friend Lazarus, and they show how near Jesus comes to our grief.

How to memorize a short verse

  1. Pick one verse for the week. Choose the one you need most right now.
  2. Put it where you look. Set it as your Lock Screen or Home Screen, and you'll read it every time you pick up your phone.
  3. Say it out loud each morning and before bed.
  4. Write it once on paper or a sticky note. Writing helps it stick.
  5. Next week, pick another, and keep reviewing the old ones.

Questions

What is the shortest verse in the Bible?

In the King James Version, John 11:35: Jesus wept.

What is a short Bible verse for strength?

Philippians 4:13: I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me. Psalm 46:1 and Psalm 121:2 are also short verses about God's strength.

What is a short Bible verse for peace?

Psalm 56:3: What time I am afraid, I will trust in thee. Also 1 Peter 5:7: Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you.

How can I memorize Bible verses?

Pick one short verse a week, keep it on your phone's Lock Screen or Home Screen, say it out loud morning and night, and keep reviewing the ones you've learned.

Which Bible verses fit on an iPhone Lock Screen?
