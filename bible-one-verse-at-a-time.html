Read the Bible one verse at a time

Narrow Gate turns the whole Bible into a calm feed. Each verse fills the screen. Swipe up for the next one, from Genesis to Revelation.

Why one verse at a time

  • Slow down and let each verse land, instead of skimming a page.
  • Swipe the way you already scroll, but through Scripture.
  • Pick up exactly where you left off.

Everything you need to read

  • Four full Bibles: KJV, WEB, BSB and ASV, all inside the app.
  • Offline: no connection and no account needed.
  • Search: jump to a reference like John 3:16, or search words like "still waters".
  • Save and share the verses that speak to you.

Questions

Is there a Bible app where you swipe one verse at a time?

Yes. Narrow Gate shows the whole Bible one verse per screen. Swipe up for the next verse.

Does the Bible work offline?

Yes. All four Bibles (KJV, WEB, BSB, ASV) are inside the app, so you can read without a connection.

Does it have the King James Version?

Yes, the KJV is included, along with the WEB, BSB and ASV.

Can I search for a verse?

Yes. Type a reference like Psalm 23 or a few words, and jump straight to the verse.
